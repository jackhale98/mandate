## DEC-108 — The causal spine: causes are bookkeeping, and the log has a rule
- **Status:** Accepted (owner, 2026-11-24, with the implementation plan's shared mechanism S1 and "I'm good with your recommendations"). Built and merged the same day (save v26). Design: `docs/design/CAUSAL_SPINE.md`.
- **Context:**
  - The owner asked for "a causal trace part of our evaluation lab and a causal graph for narrative architecture and NPC interaction".
  - Events had `causes`, but only about 4 of 18 effect kinds carried one; plant closures were explained 29% of the time, scandals and disasters never.
  - The event log kept the last 2,000 events plus every pinned one for ever, so it grew without bound.
- **Decision:**
  1. **Causes are bookkeeping at record time, never inference.** Every event names one or more causes and every effect request exactly one: an earlier event with a link (caused, continues, prompted, revealed, ended), the player's act, another character's choice, a scheduled process, a standing pressure the engine's own formula names, the world as made, or an explicit exogenous mark. Nothing in the simulation reads a cause.
  2. **Continuous effects are attributed only in the lab,** by counterfactual twins on the experiment runner. A 0.3-point change in unemployment has no cause at run time.
  3. **No planner.** Nothing plots ahead through causal links. The graph is read after the fact by "Why?" surfaces, credit and blame, the press, skip and homecoming summaries, the Legacy record, conversation callbacks and story sifting.
  4. **A skip's acts are the player's.** There is no proxy cause: a skip must leave exactly the state its turns played one by one would. Pruning therefore happens only at turn ends.
  5. **The log's retention is the spine's rule:** by age and salience (trace one year, routine four, notable twelve, landmarks for the run); live state holds what it names; nothing a reader has not yet read is pruned by age; whatever is kept keeps its ancestors; a hard cap of 6,000 events as a backstop.
  6. **Exogenous stays exogenous.** A disaster, a recession, a commodity shock and the race shock say so. A lever the director pulled is linked, never blamed, and never shown to the player.
  7. **The proof is a golden.** Eight games' event streams and states are pinned without the spine and must match with it. A commit that changes outcomes on purpose re-records them and says so.
  8. **What the player may see** through the Why query is gated like everything else: hidden events never, people only as the player knows them. Public election results count as known (a follow-up with the Why panels).
- **Consequences:** saves did not grow (a 30-year medium world went from 2,126 KB to 2,115 KB); turn time is unchanged. Plant closures name the weakest demand factor; scandals name the act behind them.
