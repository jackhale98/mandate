# Mandate — Game Vision (draft)

**Status:** Draft. Built from accepted decisions; it will grow as discovery proceeds. IDs refer to `DECISIONS.md`.

## One line
A political career roguelike. You rise from local office toward the presidency in a procedurally generated country, and you can only do what you can get others to do.

## Pillars
1. **Power through people** (DEC-010, DEC-011). Authority says what you may attempt. Mandate, Relationships, and Resources decide whether you succeed. You never set outcome sliders directly.
2. **Consequences you can walk through** (DEC-009). Each act has a walkable hub that reflects the live simulation. Big moments are staged scenes involving your actual character. Hubs persist and change while you are away.
3. **Real stakes, fair losses** (DEC-006, DEC-008). Ironman by default. Visible pressure tracks (Viability, Legal Jeopardy, Threat, Standing, Age/Health) can end the career for good, but never without warning and explanation.
4. **A different country, and different rules, every run** (DEC-007). Generated geography and society, and a constitution assembled from authored archetypes plus quirks.
5. **A climb in acts** (DEC-004, DEC-005). Four acts (Local, Regional/State, National, Presidency), made of 25–45 minute chapters that each end in a resolution. A full successful career takes 6–10 hours; a typical run, 2–5 hours.

## Tone
**Heightened realism** (DEC-061). Believable institutions and consequences, with the dramatic density of a political drama series. Calm eras and turbulent eras both exist.

## Endings
Every run closes with a Legacy epilogue: a graded summary of the career that feeds meta-progression. The summit is the **Mandate Test**, the first national verdict on your presidency as scheduled by the constitution. Terminal endings include death, prison, disqualification from office, disgrace, political wilderness, age, and retirement.

## What makes a run memorable (working hypothesis)
- People with arcs: the rival who follows you up the ladder, the mentor who calls in a debt, the constituent you failed.
- Defining tradeoffs that show up in the hub: the plant you saved, the neighborhood you priced out.
- Endings that happen *to you* on screen.
- A world and a constitution you have not seen before.
