# Parties: Majors split at the weighted median of the first principal axis; minor

**Date:** 2026-11-18
**Area:** Parties

## Parameter and change
Majors split at the weighted median of the first principal axis; minors from the tail of the next axes (was k-means)

## Evidence
k-means: one party took 65% of seats; then a centrist "minor" took 40%

## Effect
Two competitive majors; minors win a few seats
