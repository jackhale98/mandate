## Parameter and change
The canvass curve (ENGAGEMENT §2.1), per neighbourhood in the player's own memory (`PlayerState.ground`): fresh = `hood_decay` ^ visits remembered there (visits × `keep` a month); awareness gain × max(fresh, `awareness_floor`); approval gain × fit × fresh × season × same turn; race contact × same turn. `actions.canvass.approval` 0.02 → 0.012; new `hood_decay` 0.5, `hood_decay_campaign` 0.75, `keep` 0.6, `campaign_mult` 2.0 (while a candidate in an open race), `same_turn_mult` 0.5, `awareness_floor` 0.5 (the brief's start values: the grid kept them). `career.record_gain.canvass` 0.06 → 0.03; new `ask_kept` 0.05, `promise_kept` 0.08, `ask_broken` −0.04, `law_enacted` 0.08 (read from E6 and V1). Bots: the ladder's classes (single verb: canvass, media, `asks_only`, a stub until E6; blind mix: mixed, legislator, proxy; informed: explorer and three new styles; probes: idle, explore_only, corrupt), `explorer_campaigner` (fresh doorsteps where the known fit is high, asks for support, the press), `explorer_legislator` (a bill lobbied, a town hall and the press each turn), `explorer_coalition` (meets the ward, lobbies, asks residents to speak up all term); the gate ladder (§7.2) with E1's enforced rungs. Grid (16 seeds, one constant at a time from the start values; explorer − mixed in points): `approval` 0.008 / 0.012 / 0.016: canvass 68.8% / 0.558, 0.564, 0.570; explorer − mixed 1.10, 1.10, 1.13; all enforced pass. `hood_decay` 0.4 / 0.7: canvass 0.563 / 0.571, pass. `keep` 0.5 / 0.75: canvass 0.571 / 0.555; 0.75 fails `good_styles_vary` (a) (reported). `campaign_mult` 1.5 / 2.5: canvass 0.561 / 0.567, pass. `hood_decay_campaign` 0.6 / 0.9: canvass 0.556 / 0.581; 0.9 fails the enforced (b) (the campaigner is among the best in 62% of worlds: campaign doorsteps too strong). Record 0.06 with the curve: canvass 0.572 (the curve, not the record, is what moves it). The old rule emulated (approval 0.02, no fade, no season, record 0.06): canvass 87.5% / 0.630 = before, `ladder_rote` and (b) fail. Bot experiments: in a campaign, asking people met for support beats a press statement by 3 points of share for the informed styles (campaigner 0.587 → 0.620, legislator 0.600 → 0.616); the coalition saving its asks for the campaign costs 2.3 points

## Effect
Canvassing builds recognition first and fades on repeat visits; `canvass` is no longer the best routine by win rate or share; it sits below `mixed` and every informed style (only `proxy` has a lower share among the mixes). The upper rungs (`knowledge_matters`, `skill_gap`, and `ladder_learned` at + 3.0) need E6–E8's asks aimed at known concerns and V1's credit for laws: E1 is re-tuned then
