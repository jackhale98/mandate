# Knowledge effectiveness and bots (M6 K4, DEC-091): Effectiveness terms on (values as K1 proposed, one addition): lobbying

**Date:** 2026-11-19
**Area:** Knowledge effectiveness and bots (M6 K4, DEC-091)

## Parameter and change
Effectiveness terms on (values as K1 proposed, one addition): lobbying odds take a logit by how well the actor knows the target (`knowledge.effects.lobby_logit` unknown −0.5, heard −0.3, known 0, detailed +0.25; NPCs use `known`, so NPC deals are unchanged; the shown and rolled odds are one function, `npc::lobby_odds_between`); `Canvass` takes an optional worry (`issue`): its approval gain × clamp(1 + 0.5 × (salience − 1), 0.6, 1.6), salience = the neighbourhood's true felt level of that worry over the mean of its worries (`knowledge.effects.canvass`; the ceiling 1.6 is new, so one overwhelming worry cannot make a canvass several times as strong). Bots read gated facts only through `Knowing`: `legislate` ranks the menu by the ward support the player knows (the bill view's figure, coarse until learned) and drafts situational policies only when seen; lobbying picks colleagues by what the player can see (least worked on, other parties first, best shown odds; the old pick used the whip's exact leans, which carry hidden goals: the blind-invariance test caught it); canvassing names the worry the player knows a neighbourhood feels most; a campaign turn's odd slot asks ward residents the player has met for support (shown odds ≥ 0.3, each once). New strategies `explorer` (ten learning turns: every place, hello to the ward's passers-by, the ward's worries on the doorsteps, talks with people only heard of; then the mix) and `explore_only` (finds out only; never campaigns). New gates: exploring early pays (explorer ≥ mixed + 1 point of mean share, wins ≥ mixed's), exploring alone never wins (explore_only ≤ idle + 5 points and below the median), corrupt not dominant (≤ best honest + 10 points); probes (`idle`, `explore_only`) stay out of the dominance and variety checks. No content tuning beyond the canvass ceiling

## Effect
Every gate passes at 16 and 32 seeds (no dominant strategy, effort matters, idle ≤ 15%, corrupt not dominant, variety, exploring pays, exploring alone never wins). Knowledge now matters for the player-side bots (omniscient mixed gains about 0.6 points); idle is identical. Exploring pays mainly through people met (ward residents asked for support) and well-aimed canvassing; walking and talking alone change nothing at the polls (explore_only = idle within 0.1 points). The margin is thin (1.2 points against a 1-point gate): a later change that cheapens meeting people or endorsements should re-check it
