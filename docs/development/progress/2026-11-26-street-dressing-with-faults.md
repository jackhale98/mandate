# Street dressing with faults (B4)

**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Status:** built (DEC-114 §3, `BEING_THERE.md` §4.4–4.5 and §10.2's B4 row)

**The registry from state to cue.** `web/src/hub/layoutV2/dressing.ts` maps what the engine reports to what the street shows. A fault's spot (`FaultSpotView`: a kind and a slot) is a rule over the static plan: each spot kind (`pavement`, `stop`, `kerb`, `road`, `green`) lists its neighbourhood's candidate cells nearest the streets' cell first (where "See it" arrives), spaced apart, off plot land and the rows a roof can hide (a plot may be built later), and slot k is the k-th. The plan never changes for a run, so a spot never moves whatever else is open; the kinds never share a cell (terrain plus a fixed chequer, a lane split four ways, a field five ways where a neighbourhood has no streets). A street light stands on one of the plan's lamps and its cue replaces that lamp while it lasts. The cue drawn is the one the view names (`FaultView.cue`), as the art prop `cue_<cue>`; a cue with no drawing shows its status's stand-in. Cues never block walking.

**The dress pass** (`dress.ts`): `TownState.faults` (from `TownStateView.faults` via `townGeoAdapter.stateFromEngine`) adds each fault's cue to the map's props; `dressKey` includes them, so the street and the town map redress when a fault opens, starts being repaired or is fixed. Maps without faults (every pinned hash) are unchanged; `dressHash` feeds a cue's drawing only when there is one.

**Protocol:** `TownStateView.faults: FaultView[]` (the faults on the town's streets, by neighbourhood, oldest first; not rounded: on the street for anyone to see). B2's views carried faults only on a neighbourhood's place panel and on an ask; the street needs every neighbourhood's at once. No save change.

**Art** (`tools/pixelkit/art/mandate/faultCues.ts`, built into the pack's props in the works kit's legend; no colour added): 15 cues for the five fault kinds plus three stand-ins. Open, repairing and new differ in silhouette, not only colour (a test holds the three apart by shape): a dead lamp with a tag / the head open, a ladder, the council van, a cone / a new lit lantern; a flooded gully / the grate up behind a barrier and cones / a new grate in fresh concrete; a pothole / cones and a road works sign / a fresh tarmac patch; a smashed, taped shelter / a pane out and the van / a new shelter on a fresh pad with a new stop flag; a swing hanging by one chain, taped / a seat off and the van / fresh paint on a new safety surface. Vans have a mirrored look the street picks to keep the van off a house front. Review sheet: `node --import ./bin/hooks.mjs art/mandate/faultSheets.ts`. Goldens: the 21 new props added, none changed; `PACK_VERSION` unchanged (no existing look changed).

**Tests.** `layoutV2/dressing.test.ts` over the engine-dumped towns of eight run seeds: every slot of every fault kind in `faults.jsonc` has a cell in every neighbourhood, inside it, walkable, never shared, stable; open, repairing and new show three different cues on that cell and move nothing else (terrain, walking, buildings, other props but the lamp); every fault at once keeps `checkTownV2` clean. `tools/pixelkit/test/faultCues.test.ts`: every content cue drawn, silhouettes apart, palette, cell, size. `web/e2e/fault-cues.spec.ts`: a real world (seed requirement `fault_seen_open_repairing_and_fixed`, `mandate-seeds`: one fault of the player's town seen open, being repaired and repaired at turns' ends within 10 turns), the street waits for each reported fault's own cue on its spot and photographs the spot in each state, desktop and phone (390 × 844). The probe `window.__townPlan.state` carries the turn's faults.

**Left:** works projects on the street (the engine has `policy/works.rs` but no street view of works; the registry takes them when one exists, drawing with `composeWorks`); a permanent bus shelter and play area where no fault is (the plan places none, so the shelter shows only while its fault is in the view); faults on the town map overview; cues at dusk (the dead lamp at night is already dark).
